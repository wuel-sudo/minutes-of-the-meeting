At the end of the lesson the students should be able to:

  3. Construct a simple set of minutes based on a given scenario using the appropriate format.
Lesson discussion

What are meeting minutes?

Meeting minutes are the written record of what was discussed and decided during a meeting.

They typically include:

Date and time
Attendees
Topics discussed
Decisions made
Action items
Time of adjournment

Its purpose is to:

  • Provide an official account of the meeting.
  • Keep track of decisions and next steps.
  • Remind members of their assigned tasks.
  • Inform members who were unable to attend.
Lesson discussion

Parts of the Meeting

1. Meeting Details
Name of the organization, date, time, and venue.
2. Attendance
Members who were present and absent.
3. Call to Order
When the meeting officially begins.
4. Approval of Previous Minutes
Review and approval of the previous meeting’s minutes, if applicable.
5. Agenda and Discussion
Topics discussed during the meeting.
6. Decisions and Action Items
Agreements, assigned tasks, responsible persons, and deadlines.
7. Adjournment
When the meeting ends and details of the next meeting, if available.
8. Prepared by and Approved by
Persons who prepared and approved the minutes, when applicable.
